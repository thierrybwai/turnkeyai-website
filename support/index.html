Common questions

Before you open a ticket.

How fast do you reply?

First reply within one business day, every time. Most tickets are resolved within the same email thread over a day or two. Anything truly urgent? Call out "urgent" in the form and email start@tkai.com.au directly.

Does it cost anything?

No. Support is included with every deployment. There's no subscription, no incident fee, no surprise invoice. If a fix needs new work (e.g. a brand-new workflow), we'll quote it before starting — never after.

Do you access my Mac Mini or VPS to fix things?

Only if you ask us to and only with credentials you generate. By default, all troubleshooting happens by email: we describe the fix, you apply it or we screen-share. Nothing is remote-controlled behind your back.

What kinds of issues do you handle?

Anything related to your deployment: workflow failures, integration breakages (Xero, MYOB, Slack, Telegram), prompt tuning, API key rotations, performance drops, "how do I" questions, and small workflow improvements. If it's outside scope, we'll say so clearly.

What if I just want to add a new workflow?

Open a ticket and describe what you want it to do. We'll quote it (typically one to three hours of work) and turn it around within a week of acceptance. New workflows are billed separately from support.

Who answers the tickets?

The TurnkeyAI engineering team — the same people who deployed your system. No outsourced helpdesks, no chatbots replying first. Every reply comes from a human who has access to your deployment notes.

Tell us what's broken. We'll fix it.

One form, one human reply, one thread until it's resolved. No portal, no subscription.
