Yes, every hour of the day. TurnkeyAI deploys an autonomous AI agent that replies to booking questions, confirms reservations, handles check-in queries, and coordinates cleaners between stays. It covers nights and weekends so late enquiries get answered while you sleep. It can reply in English, French, Mandarin, and German.

How many hours can a hotel or rental operator save?

Most operators win back the front-desk hours lost to repetitive messages. Across 50+ Australian SME deployments, the average client saves $1,000 to $2,000 a week and breaks even in about three weeks. The largest gains come from instant guest replies and automatic review responses on every stay.

Yes. The agent works with the systems you already run, including Hostaway, Guesty, Cloudbeds, Mews, Airbnb, and Booking.com. It pulls messages from every channel into one place and replies or escalates. You direct it in plain English over Slack or Telegram, with no new platform for staff to learn.
