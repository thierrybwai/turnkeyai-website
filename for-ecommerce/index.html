Common questions

AI for e-commerce, answered.

Can AI handle order enquiries, returns, and refunds?

Yes, the high-volume parts. TurnkeyAI deploys an autonomous agent that answers order and shipping enquiries, processes returns and refunds, replies to supplier emails, and chases reviews. It also follows up on abandoned carts. Refunds and edge cases wait for your approval.

Does it really respond to customers 24/7?

Yes, around the clock, every day. The agent replies to "where is my order" and returns questions at 2am the same way it does at 2pm. Across 50+ Australian SME deployments, the average client saves $1,000 to $2,000 per week and breaks even within roughly three weeks.

Does it integrate with Shopify, WooCommerce, Gorgias, and Klaviyo?

Yes. The agent connects to the tools you already run, including Shopify, WooCommerce, Gorgias, Klaviyo, and your support email. You operate it in plain English over Slack or Telegram. No replatforming and no new software for your team to learn.

How long until it's running, and what does it cost?

Live in 7 business days, from $2,999 AUD one-time. The Cloud option ($2,999) runs on a dedicated server we manage with 3 workflows. The Mac Mini option ($5,999) installs an Apple Mac Mini M4 in your office, yours to keep, with 5 workflows. No subscription. Both include 2 weeks of support.

Is my store and customer data secure?

On your own hardware. Your data, logs, and store credentials stay on your Mac Mini or your dedicated VPS, not a shared cloud. The agent sends prompts to Anthropic's Claude API to run the model; Anthropic does not train on API data. Sensitive actions like refunds wait for your one-tap approval.
