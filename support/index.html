How it works

5 steps from ticket to resolution.

Our support agent does the heavy lifting. You stay in control of every change applied to your system.

01

Submit a ticket

Describe the problem in plain English. Tell us how urgent it is and how we can reach your Mac Mini.

02

Agent reads your system

Through your existing OpenClaw extension, our support agent pulls logs, config, and health metrics. No SSH credentials needed.

03

Diagnosis & draft fix

The agent identifies the issue and drafts the exact fix. You see it, the engineer sees it, in plain English.

04

Apply with your OK

Routine fixes apply automatically with an audit log. Destructive changes (config writes, restarts) wait for your one-click approval.

05

Resolution + receipt

You receive a summary email: what broke, what we did, audit log, and how to prevent it next time. Average: 2 business hours.

Security

No SSH credentials. No raw shell access. No surprises.

Your Mac Mini already runs our OpenClaw runtime. We extend it with a support-agent target that exposes a whitelisted operations API: read logs, restart a workflow, preview a config change, fetch health metrics.

Anything destructive (config write, system command, restart) requires your one-click approval in Slack or by email. Every action is logged locally on your Mac Mini, with a copy in your inbox the moment the ticket closes.

Common questions

Things people ask before subscribing.

Do you need SSH access to my Mac Mini?

No. We use the existing OpenClaw runtime already on your machine. We add a support-agent target with a whitelisted API. SSH credentials are never requested, stored, or used.

What if I subscribe and never need support?

Your monthly health check still runs. It catches drift, prevents outages, and we apply small improvements to your workflows. Most subscribers' biggest savings come from prevention, not repairs.

What kinds of issues can your agent fix?

API key rotations, workflow timeouts, integration breakages (Xero, MYOB, Slack, etc.), prompt tuning, log analysis, performance issues. If something truly novel comes up, our human engineer takes it from there at no extra charge for subscribers.

Is my data secure during a support session?

Yes. The support agent operates only via the whitelisted API. No data leaves your Mac Mini except for what's necessary to diagnose (logs, config snippets). Every command is logged and the audit trail is emailed to you the moment the ticket closes.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your billing portal in 30 seconds. No penalty, no exit interview. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Who handles the support behind the scenes?

Our support agent (powered by Claude Sonnet 4.5) handles 80% of tickets autonomously, with our engineers reviewing destructive changes and edge cases. For complex issues, a human engineer takes over directly.
